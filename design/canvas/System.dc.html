Identity is the icon tile and the label ink, both in the topic hue. A topic looks the same wherever it appears, card or tile.

The sentence stays in the same ink on every card, so hue never competes with reading.

A recessed footer carries the count and the way in. Structure inside one card replaces adding another card style.

Rules

Cards are white on a warm-white ground, 1.13 to 1 apart. Hue never fills a card — it marks a 26px tile and the label above the sentence.

Exactly one element per screen carries emphasis, and it is the hero. Emphasis is a filled ground, not another tint.

Amatic sets the little headers and nothing else. It has no ā ī ū or ʻokina, so no line that can carry a Hawaiian place name is ever set in it — which is also why headlines moved to Nunito.

Notices carry no hue. A card with nothing to act on should not look like one that does.

Danger and warning keep the brick and amber they already have. The topic hues are chosen to stay clear of both.

This still bends your rule that colour appears only in pictures and danger blocks, but only as far as a tile and a label. That is the one thing here you have to decide.
