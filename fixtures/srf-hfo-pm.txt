.DISCUSSION...
A High Surf Advisory (HSA) remains in effect for north and west 
facing shores of Niihau, Kauai, Oahu, and Molokai, and north facing 
shores of Maui, through tonight. The current northwest swell will
decline tonight, with surf falling below advisory criteria by early 
Friday. Surf will continue to decrease throughout the day on Friday, 
but will experience another small bump on Saturday with a new north- 
northwest swell, followed by a more significant north swell which 
could push surf back to advisory levels on Sunday.

Surf along east-facing shores will remain rough and choppy due to
the strong trade winds through Friday. East-facing shores may decrease
briefly as trades begin to ease early this weekend, but will
increase again Sunday into early next week as trades increase
again. South shore surf will tiny to small for the next few days.
