.DISCUSSION...

PacIOOS Buoy 51201 near Waimea Bay observed the peak of the large,  
north-northwest swell late last evening at nearly 11 feet with a 15 
second period. A High Surf Advisory (HSA) remains in effect for 
north- facing shores of Kauai, Oahu, Molokai and Maui and west- facing
shores of Kauai and Oahu through this afternoon. The large swell will
generally maintain through the morning before declining this 
afternoon and falling below advisory criteria tonight. Surf will 
continue to decrease Friday, but will see a small bump on Saturday
with a new north-northwest swell, followed by a more significant 
north swell which could push surf back to advisory levels on Sunday.

Surf along east-facing shores will remain rough and choppy due to
the strong trade winds through Friday, then may diminish slightly
this weekend. A small, long-period south swell may bring a small 
bump to south shore surf today. Surf becomes tiny again Friday into 
the weekend for south-facing shores.
